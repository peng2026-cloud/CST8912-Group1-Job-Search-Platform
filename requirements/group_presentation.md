# Group Presentation

## Purpose

Please prepare an iterative presentation in alignment with the research assignment. This is a group task, and the groups will remain the same as those for the Group Research Assignment.

## Learning Outcomes

- Work effectively and collaboratively in multi-generational and multi-cultural environments.
- Communicate with group members using groupware (Zoom and MS Teams).
- Complete and define group contracts, logs, and agendas for effective team meetings
- Demonstrate business acumen, present a professional appearance and communication style and act as an ambassador for an organization.
- Adapt language and communication style to ensure understanding by a diverse range of people
- Use respectful verbal, nonverbal, written, and electronic communications for a variety of work situations

## Instructions

Your group will create a presentation that highlights/summarizes the progress of your research assignment. The presentations will be shared with the whole class in theory sessions.

## Presentation Requirements

- Introductory slide with name of topic and presenters
- Minimum 5 slides of content, maximum 10
- No more than 20 words per slide, approximately 20-point font
- At least one image, chart, picture, or graphic per slide
- One slide contains a quiz to test viewers on what they just learned (5-10 questions)
- Bibliography slide referencing sources, image credits and any textbooks used that are in addition to the ones from the written report. You do not need to duplicate references

## Deliverables

- Progress of the research assignment
- Cloud Architecture chosen along with cloud services
- Recommendation of cloud services and which is better than other in terms of cost and performance
- Key challenges faced when implementing the solution
- Key cloud architectural components used in every phase of the project
- Lessons learned
