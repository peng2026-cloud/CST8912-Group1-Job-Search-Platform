# Group Final Project

## Purpose

In a group of four, students must create a proof of concept to determine the key trends in cloud solutioning based on architectural designs to evaluate the cloud applications efficiently.
The project must be on a use case.
When you find a use case you are interested in, look for a reference architecture that engages with that use case.

## Learning Outcomes

- Work effectively and collaboratively in multi-generational and multi-cultural environments.
- Communicate with group members using groupware (Zoom and MS Teams).
- Complete and define group contracts, logs, and agendas for effective team meetings
- Demonstrate business acumen, present a professional appearance and communication style and act as an ambassador for an organization.
- Adapt language and communication style to ensure understanding by a diverse range of people
- Use respectful verbal, nonverbal, written, and electronic communications for a variety of work situations

## Instructions

Create a cloud computing proof of concept solution using a reference architecture of your choice and document different cloud solutions and best practices as discussed in the class.

Emphasis should be on the connectivity from the front-end to the backend and incorporation of different cloud architectural components.

## Written Report

### Layout:

Text must be a minimum of 15 full pages and a maximum of 20 full pages.
Visual aids do not count towards the total pages unless they contribute valuable additional information that would not be covered effectively as text, such as a chart.
Include visual aids if they are appropriate and properly referenced in your report.
You must reference the source of any research, pictures, text or aids.
Use a conclusion to summarize your findings.

### Formatting:

The font must be 12-point font.
The body of the report should be spaced at 1.15.
Any long quotes or lists should be single-spaced.
The title page will include the report title, the names of your group members, date, course code with section and Professor.
If you want to make it beautiful, add an appropriate image or background.
In the body of the report, use subheadings to properly break your report into sections and subsections and number all pages.
Include a table of contents so that your report is easy to navigate.

### Appearance:

Report should look clean and professional.
See rubric below for essential elements.
In order to produce a seamless report one or two people will have to edit the final report so that the “voice” is consistent and there is no overlap in information.
You are responsible to and for one another.
Check each other’s work for content, references and errors.
Everyone shares the mark so you will want to share the editing responsibilities.

### References:

You must reference all words and thoughts that are not your own or you may receive a zero on your assignment when the report is sent to the Academic Integrity Arbiter.
Refer to [Academic Policy AA48 Academic Integrity](https://documentcloud.adobe.com/link/review?uri=urn:aaid:scds:US:4d9f0f97-28c4-4327-adb6-908a12024c78) for more information.

- If you wish to quote a sentence or a term from a website, blog or article, place the content in quotation marks, identify the author and date in brackets, and then list the source in the bibliography/reference page at the end of the document.
- If you wish to use someone’s concept but you are using your own words or summarizing it (e.g. a list of categories would be someone else’s even if you are using your own words or examples) you would not use quotation marks but you would still have to reference whose concept this is.
- Your bibliography/reference page is a collection of all sources that were used and it must be arranged alphabetically.
  Only list those sources that actually influenced the content of your report (any additional references are unnecessary padding).
